The Itinerary

Wednesday, September 30 Optional Pre-Reunion Day ▾
All Day
This is the day before our gathering begins — entirely optional, but a wonderful opportunity if you're arriving early.

September 30 is Canada's National Day for Truth and Reconciliation — a provincial holiday in BC — and Victoria will host a Powwow organized by the Songhees Nation.

Powwows are joyful celebrations of Indigenous culture: music, dance, regalia, and food, open and welcoming to all. It would be a rich and memorable way to learn about the first peoples of Vancouver Island before our week begins.

Victoria is a two-and-a-bit hour drive south from Parksville. You would need to arrange your own transport — either a rental car or driving down together with others who are arriving early.

Plan to arrive by 12:00 noon for the Grand Entry of dancers — the ceremonial opening procession that begins the Powwow.

🟠 Wear orange if you can. September 30 is also Orange Shirt Day, honouring survivors of residential schools and acknowledging the ongoing impact of that history on Indigenous communities across Canada.

Good to Know

🌦

Weather

Early October can be beautiful and sunny (high teens °C) or cool and rainy. Pack a fleece or light puffer jacket and a raincoat. Bring an extra pair of shoes for water activities.

🦅

Wildlife

Expect seabirds, eagles, seals, sea lions, and whales. Possible sea otters and dolphins. On shore: oysters, clams, sea stars, sand dollars, sea anemones. Deer are ubiquitous on land — take care when driving. Bears and cougars are very unlikely.

Check before harvesting any shellfish — rules apply on when and where.

💸

Taxes & Tipping

  • Grocery food: no sales tax
  • Most purchases: +12% (5% GST + 7% BC PST)
  • Alcohol: +15% tax; sold in special stores only
  • Gas: currently over $2/litre, taxes included
  • Restaurant tips: 15% minimum, 18% now common
  • Uber/taxi: 10% typical
🚗

Getting Around

Uber is available in Parksville but not at the airport. Taxis are available. Public transit is very limited. Beth and Lisa, Patty, Maureen and Rahul, Rocky and Richard, and Alison and Gordon are all bringing their cars, so we'll be able to manage local transportation ourselves.

🍁

Culture Notes

Want a flavour of life here? Subscribe to the local CHEK News newsletter (sign up at the bottom of their page).

First-time visitors to Canada: it's very much like the US (outside Quebec), but there are differences. This etiquette guide is a reasonable primer — and there's a chapter on anti-American sentiment (written pre-Trump, mind you).
