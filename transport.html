By Air

✈️
Flight options into the island
Fly from Vancouver (YVR) to Nanaimo (YCD) on Air Canada or WestJet. See the flight schedule into YCD. Note: these are tiny planes — the flight is only 11 minutes in the air, and there is room for only very small carry-on bags in the cabin. 🚗 Arriving Oct 1? We'll pick you up.
Fly from various locations into Victoria (YYJ), then rent a car for the two-hour drive north to Parksville.
Seaplanes depart from Vancouver Airport and Vancouver Harbour, arriving into Nanaimo Harbour. A Helijet service is also available. 🚗 Arriving Oct 1? We'll pick you up.

By Sea

⛴️
Ferry options across the water
Take the passenger-only Black Ball Coho Ferry from Seattle to Victoria, then rent a car for the drive north.
Take the passenger-only Hullo Ferry from Vancouver Harbour to Nanaimo Harbour. 🚗 Arriving Oct 1? We'll pick you up.
Take the passenger-and-car BC Ferry from Vancouver Tsawwassen Terminal (south of Vancouver, closest to the US border) to Nanaimo Duke Point Terminal. 🚗 Arriving on foot Oct 1? We'll pick you up.
Take the passenger-and-car BC Ferry from Vancouver Horseshoe Bay Terminal (north of Vancouver, furthest from the US border) to Nanaimo Departure Bay Terminal. 🚗 Arriving on foot Oct 1? We'll pick you up.

Local Transport

🚗
Getting around once you're here
Several friends are bringing their cars — Beth and Lisa, Patty, Maureen and Rahul, and Alison and Gordon — and we'll rent an additional vehicle to help with local transport throughout the week.
Public transportation is very limited in this area. Taxis are available at the airport and ferry terminals. Uber is permitted in Parksville, but not elsewhere in this part of the island.
